# Morning Check-in

Morning ritual to ground yourself and set intentions for the day.

---

## Step 1: Morning Questions

Ask these questions one by one (wait for answers):

### Q1: How are you feeling this morning?
- Energy level: 1-5
- Describe it: tired/ok/energized/anxious/excited/blank

### Q2: What's the weather like?
- Sunny/cloudy/rainy
- (Sometimes external conditions affect internal state)

### Q3: If you could only accomplish ONE thing today, what would it be?
- (This helps anchor to what truly matters)

### Q4: What do you need most right now?
- Energy/company/quiet/stimulation/rest/clarity

---

## Step 2: Diagnose Current State

Based on their answers, identify the pattern:

- **Low energy + bad weather** → Double whammy, adjust expectations
- **Anxious/overthinking** → Need to externalize thoughts
- **Energized** → Capitalize on momentum
- **Lonely/isolated** → Need connection
- **Unclear priorities** → Need to clarify goals

---

## Step 3: Suggest Focus

Based on their state, suggest what kind of day this should be:

1. Acknowledge their current state
2. Suggest 2-3 options for how to approach the day
3. Explain why (core logic)
4. Ask: "Which feels right? Or how do you want to adjust?"

**Examples**:
- Low energy? → "Maybe focus on 1-2 important things, don't overload"
- Anxious? → "Let's write down what's on your mind first, then decide priorities"
- Energized? → "Great day to tackle that hard thing you've been avoiding"

---

## Step 4: Record to Daily File

After they choose, update `memory/timeline/daily/{today}.md` with a Morning Check-in section:

```markdown
## Morning Check-in

**Energy**: X/5
**Feeling**: [their description]
**Weather**: [sunny/cloudy/rainy]
**Top priority**: [the ONE thing]
**Need**: [energy/company/etc]

**Today's approach**: [what they decided]
```

---

## Step 5: Remind Core Principles

Optionally remind them of principles that help:
- Start with the most important thing
- It's okay to adjust plans based on energy
- Progress > perfection

---

## Output Format

1. Ask the 4 questions
2. Summarize their answers
3. Identify pattern + give suggestions
4. They choose, you record
5. Optionally commit the update

---

**Key Principles**:
- **Engaging**: Ask questions to activate thinking, not a mechanical checklist
- **Adaptive**: Adjust recommendations based on their state
- **Respectful**: If they're really low, lower expectations, don't push
- **Grounded**: Base suggestions on what actually helps them
